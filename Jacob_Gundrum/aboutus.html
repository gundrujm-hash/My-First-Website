About Us

Miami Unvifersity Force Sensitives or MU Force Sensitives, is a club that is based around in a love of everything Star Wars. From its beginning In a galaxy far far away, Star Wars has captured the eyes,ears, and hearts of countless people worldwide. We decided to bring this galaxy far far away, really really close, to Miami University's campus. But even after nearly 50 years from the debut of its first movie there are some who still do not know its impact. So, along with giving veteran fans a space to share their love, it is also a place for newcomers to discover this amazing work of media. And each Monday night from 7-8pm we bring the life to Star Wars and spread it throught Miami's campus. To quote Obi Wan Kenobi, the Force is, "an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds us and penetrates us; it binds the galaxy together". Our mission is to build the living force, to build community.
